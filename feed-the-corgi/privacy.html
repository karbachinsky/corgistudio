Privacy Policy

Feed the Corgi · last updated 2026-10-01

Feed the Corgi collects nothing, sends nothing and asks for nothing. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party services inside the app. It works with the network switched off.

What the app stores

The game keeps your child's progress on the device itself, in the app's own storage:

  • how many meals have been cooked, and how many stars were earned and spent
  • which wardrobe items have been bought and which the corgi is wearing
  • the last kitchen that was open, and whether sound is muted

None of this is personal information, none of it identifies anyone, and none of it leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no backup on our side, because there is no "our side" — we never receive it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect names, email addresses, photos, contacts or location.
  • It does not use advertising, and shows no ads of any kind.
  • It contains no in-app purchases and no payment of any kind.
  • It uses no analytics or crash-reporting services from third parties.
  • It requests no permissions — no camera, no microphone, no location, no notifications.
  • It contains no links that lead a child out of the app, and no social features.

Children

This game is made for young children, and it is built so that the question of their data does not arise: nothing about them is collected, stored remotely or shared. Because no personal information is ever collected, there is nothing for a parent to consent to, review or delete on a server. This is our reading of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and of the corresponding rules in the UK and the European Union.

Crash reports

If you have switched on Apple's "Share with App Developers" setting on your device, Apple may send us anonymous crash diagnostics through App Store Connect. That data comes from Apple, is not linked to any person, and is controlled by your own iOS settings — you can turn it off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Changes

If a future version of the game ever collects anything, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here rather than buried.
