Feed the Corgi — Support

A cooking game for two, three and four year olds

Something not working, or an idea for a dish? Write to igorkarbachinsky@gmail.com — a real person reads it.

How the game works

  • The corgi dreams of a dish in the bubble above its head. Drag those ingredients from the belt into the plate or the pot.
  • A soup has to be stirred with the ladle before it is finished. Drag a finger round the pot.
  • A burger and a hot dog are closed with their lid, and the lid goes on last.
  • Every finished meal pays stars. Tap the little wardrobe at the top of the menu to spend them on costumes — trying anything on is free.

Common questions

My child cannot read. Is that a problem? No. The game has no instructions to read, and nothing in it needs an adult to explain. If a child sits still for a while, a helping hand shows what to do.

Can they lose or get stuck? No. There are no timers and no way to fail. An ingredient that does not belong simply wiggles and goes back to the belt.

Are there ads or purchases? None of either, anywhere in the game.

Progress disappeared. Progress lives on the device and is removed if the app is deleted or the device is reset. There are no accounts, so it cannot be restored from our side — if this happened unexpectedly, please write and tell us what you did just before, which helps us find the bug.

The sound is off. The speaker button at the top right of the menu mutes and unmutes everything. The game also respects the phone's silent switch.

Privacy

The game collects nothing and sends nothing. Details on the privacy page.
